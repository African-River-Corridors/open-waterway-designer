- **A fairway fit that kinks at a join is no longer taken; the plain line stays there.** Before, on a synthetic sine river (wavelength 1,200 m, amplitude 600 m, banks 125 m either side of the centre sine, R_min 255 m, step 10 m, two passes), the water-aware line had a radius of 180.5 m on the default chord (core, centreline built from the banks) against 255.0 m for the plain line; through the app's document path (CEMT_IV, 253.4 m for the plain line) it was 177.9 m. After: 255.0 m in the core case and 253.4 to 253.7 m through the document path (the plain line's 253.4 m, or within 0.4 m of it). With banks 150 m either side the document path goes from 178 m to 253.3 to 253.5 m. A line where the fit's joins were fine does not move; `--use-water false` does not move. The repository's own cases (the use-water test river, the web example river at CEMT_Va) do not move: the full test suite, with its recorded numbers, passes unchanged.
