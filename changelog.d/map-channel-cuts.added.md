- **The channel and its cut on the map, before and after (MAP-5).** The web app's map now draws the design channel itself, not only its centre line, and the cut the volumes assumed. Two new ticks in the Layers list: **Channel** (on at first) draws the channel bed as a pale band between its two edges, and the edges at the water surface as thin lines; **Cut extent** (off at first; greyed until the volumes have run with the DEM loaded) draws the daylight lines, where the cut slopes meet the existing ground, and colours the ground between them by the depth of cut: dredge (inside the river polygon) in blues and dry cut in oranges, darker with depth, in six classes (0, 1, 2, 5, 10 and 20 m and more) with a key in the legend. A click on the cut reads the point out: chainage and offset, ground level before, level after, depth of cut, dredge or dry, and the nearer station's cut areas and face height. In 3D, and whenever the terrain shading or the contours are on, a new control chooses the ground the map shows: **Before** (the DEM as it is) or **After** (the DEM with the channel cut into it: the bed, the slopes below water, the berm and the dry slopes up to the ground, so a tall face is a real slope in the terrain). **Compare** shows the other ground while it is held (pointer, Space or Enter). The terrain shading, the contours and the cut-depth colours follow the ground shown. The engine derives all of it on demand (`owd_format::design::channel`): the stations are the volumes stage's stored ones, the cut face is the one rule the volumes use (`cut_model_a_face`), and the daylight offsets are the stored top of cut moved out to the exact crossing with the ground. Before the volumes have run, the channel is drawn from the same station frame, half-widths and water levels with no terrain read. The After ground is a second terrain state of the same tile maker (`TerrainState`, `Overlay` in `terrain_tile`; address `owd-dem://{id}/after/{cut}/…`), read point by point from the stations and a small index of the segments between them: under 3 MB for 8,000 stations whatever the size of the DEM, made in the worker when the design changes. Nothing is stored in the design: the design file and every export are unchanged. The choice of ground and the ticks are remembered on this machine (browser storage). The desktop app does not have these layers yet: their ticks say "web app only for now".
