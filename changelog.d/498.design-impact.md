- **The rolling-circle line moves where one bend turns into the next, and now holds R_min there.** A circle that used to fall back to the crossing exit at a reverse bend (no tangent exit, because the old line turns the other way past the touch) now takes the reverse exit: arc, inner common tangent, arc of R_min turning the other way. The line loses the corner between the two bends, and its measured radius on the default chord rises to R_min. On a synthetic chain of 120 m bends of 120 degrees, rolled at R_min 240 m (step 10 m, two passes, both sides), the measured radius on the 80 m chord rises from 91.0 m to 240.0 m and the line shortens from 1,708.2 m to 1,673.6 m. On a sine centreline of wavelength 700 m and amplitude 120 m with straight ends, the radius rises from 129.5 m to 240.0 m and the line shortens from 4,194.4 m to 3,979.0 m. A line where every circle already took the tangent exit does not move: the default synthetic river at R_min 240, 255 and 300 m gives the same line, vertex for vertex.
