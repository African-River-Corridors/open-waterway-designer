- **The pool lattice no longer keeps a baseline segment that breaks R_min in place of a lattice line that holds it.** A pool kept its baseline when the lattice line cost more, though the baseline (the rolling-circle line or the centreline) is cheap just because it hugs a tight bend. Now a dearer lattice line is replaced by the baseline only when the baseline holds R_min, or the lattice line does not either. `details.n_replaced` and the CLI line count the pools whose lattice line is in the option, not the pools that converged. Each pool record has `used` and, when its baseline was kept, `kept_baseline_because`.
