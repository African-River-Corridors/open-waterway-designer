- **Drawings and reports read the volumes stage's stored stations (spec 0017, ticket 10).** The long section, the cross sections, the plan sheet and the quantity schedule read `stages.volumes.results.sections` through one function, `Session::section_stations`, which the CLI, the report and the web app all call. While the volumes record is fresh and holds what a sheet needs, nothing walks the terrain again. A sheet that needs a section profile the record did not keep (a cross-section sheet at another spacing, or a station picked in the app) walks the line again on the DEM and says so in the sheet's warnings; with no DEM it refuses. A neighbouring station's profile is never drawn as the one asked for. A stale record is refused as before; a fresh record saved before the stations were stored is walked again and says so. Each stored station now also keeps its top of cut left and right, which the plan sheet draws at every station (`schema/stages/volumes.1.schema.json`). On the web example every sheet and every report is the same, byte for byte. No design number moves.
