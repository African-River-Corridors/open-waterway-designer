- **The river-following line, an alignment method (OPT-4).** `owd align --method river-following` builds the design line from the river: arcs and straights that follow the centreline at R_min (tangent everywhere, a vessel length of straight between tight opposite arcs, bends tighter than R_min held and recorded), meander cut-throughs costed with the volumes stage's own section and taken by a stated rule or forced by id, and a sideways shift to cut less with the shifted line's curvature as a hard limit. Every length comes from the sizing, so a change of vessel or rule quality rebuilds the line on `owd run`. The app lists the cut-throughs with a force on/off choice, the forced stretches and each bend.
