- **The lattice search reads no clock.** `owd_core::optimise::lattice::solve_lattice_until` takes a `stop` check from the caller, asked once in every 1000 expansions; `solve_lattice` is that search with no limit. `LatticeConfig::wall_cap_s` and `LatticeResult::seconds` are gone, so two runs of one search give equal results, and `owd-core` no longer needs `web-time`. The evaluation harness refuses a case that sets `wall_cap_s`.
