- **A pool or piece that says its goal is closed now ends on its goal.** A search state is a snapped position and heading; when one is reached again more cheaply its pose is replaced, and the line, rebuilt by running the primitives forward from the start, could then end some metres from the pose its closing path was computed from. The closing path was appended as it was, so the line ended off its goal, in line with it, while the result said the goal was closed, and the joined line had a gap and a bend far tighter than R_min at that join. The closing path is now computed again from where the line really ends, and the line costed as rebuilt; when no closing path leaves from there, the result says the goal is not closed and the pool's end is moved as for any unclosed pool. With water that costs nothing to pass through, no state is reached again more cheaply at weight 0, so the fault showed with a length weight above 0. On a synthetic 16 km sine river at weight 5, one piece said closed and ended 20.8 m off its goal; it now ends on it.
