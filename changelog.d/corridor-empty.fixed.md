- **A pool is no longer left unsearched because its corridor came out empty (OPT-3).** The corridor of a pool is every point within `corridor_half_m` of its baseline, taken as the buffer of the baseline. For some lines with a corridor wider than the line's tightest bend (300 m round a bend of radius 180 m in the test) that buffer came out empty: the search grid covered nothing, the search stopped after one expansion, and the pool kept its baseline as "the search did not converge". The corridor is now checked: when the buffer does not hold the baseline's own points it is built again as the union of the buffers of short stretches of the line. A pool whose corridor still cannot be built keeps its baseline with the reason "the corridor round the baseline could not be built", and the design line carries a warning that names the pool. A buffer that holds the baseline is used as before, so no other result changes.
