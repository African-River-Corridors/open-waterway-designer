- **The weir search is practical with a wide lift menu.** The search read the cut table's running totals afresh for every (site, site, level) and rounded every (level, lift) for every pair of sites. It now works both out once. The cascades and their costs are the same, bit for bit. On a synthetic 240 km river with a candidate site every 500 m and lifts of 1 to 12 m in 1 m steps (73 pool levels), the search itself takes about 0.3 s where it took over 30 s; the flood table for the same river takes about 3.5 s to build.
