- **Run inputs: objective, limits and target level (spec 0017, ticket 11).** A design can state what its run aims at (one design measure to minimise or maximise), what it must stay within (design measures, each with an at-most or at-least bound; a bound on cost names its currency), and the output level it aims to reach (concept, pre-feasibility or feasibility). They are stored as `inputs.run`, typed, with a schema (`schema/stages/inputs.run.1.schema.json`), and set by `owd run-inputs` and by the web app's run-input fields through the same function. A measure, sense or level OWD does not know is refused by name (`RUN_MEASURE_UNKNOWN`, `RUN_SENSE_UNKNOWN`, `RUN_LEVEL_UNKNOWN`), as are a cost bound with no currency, two bounds of one kind on a measure, and bounds no design can meet. Nothing optimises on them yet, and no stage goes stale when they change. A design without them opens and saves as before: no objective, no limits, and the target is concept.
