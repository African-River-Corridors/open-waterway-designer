- **Typed alignment settings.** The alignment stage's settings are a type, `owd_format::design::AlignmentSettings`, with one typed part per method (`rolling_circle`, `reroute`). A setting a method does not know, from the caller or in the document, is refused by name before the stage looks for terrain; a re-route setting given to the rolling circle is refused. `owd align --help` reads the defaults of `--step`, `--passes`, `--corridors` and `--fairness-weight` from the type.
