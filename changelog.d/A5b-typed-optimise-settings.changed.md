- **Typed optimise settings.** The optimise stage's settings are a type, `owd_format::design::OptimiseSettings`. A setting the stage does not know, from the caller or in the document, is refused by name before the stage looks for terrain or does any work. `owd optimise --help` reads the defaults of `--sites`, `--lifts`, `--wet-weight` and `--dry-weight` from the type; `k_best` and `n_heading` are whole-number fields with no hidden fallback.
