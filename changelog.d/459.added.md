- **The channel limits as an export.** `owd export DOC --geojson --limits [--wgs84]` writes the left and right channel limits of each design line option: the edges of the channel bottom, from the same widened half-width as the volumes cut, at stations every 25 m. The app shell gives the same text (`AppState::export_limits_geojson`). The limits are worked out when exported, never stored.
