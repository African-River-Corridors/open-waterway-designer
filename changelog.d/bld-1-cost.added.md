- **A cost per building flooded in the weir search (BLD-1).** `building_cost` (`owd optimise --building-cost X`, or the Optimise row in the app) is the engineer's price for each building a pool floods, such as a resettlement cost. A building counts when its point is in land the pool floods by the weir search's own rule and the water there is at least `building_min_depth_m` deep (`--building-min-depth`, default 0.3 m); buildings below the confidence or area thresholds are counted apart, never costed. The count is a table built once, like the flooded hectares (240 km, 40,000 buildings, 479 sites and 73 levels: 0.5 ms for the table). Off (0) by default: the search is then unchanged, byte for byte. A cost with no buildings loaded, or with no DEM, is refused in plain words. With buildings loaded the flood report (`--flood-report`) gives the buildings and the settlement land each pool floods, the flooded buildings by confidence band, and the first 20 by place.
