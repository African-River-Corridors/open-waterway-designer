- **One design line per run, and document format 0.5 (spec 0017, ticket 6).** The document no longer holds a list of design line options. The alignment stage stores one typed design line (`stages.alignment.results`, schema `schema/stages/alignment.1.schema.json`): its line, its method and settings, its checks, its bends (chainage from and to, least radius, R_min, pass) and its cut-offs (where it leaves the river's water, islands counted as land, with the river chainage it leaves and rejoins at). The method is a setting (`stages.alignment.settings.method`), and `owd run` runs it again. A second method, or `owd design-line`, replaces the line. The pool lattice stores its refined line in `stages.optimise.results.design_line`, and the volumes then read that line only; the volumes results are that line's row. `owd align`, `owd optimise`, `owd volumes`, `owd run` and `owd status` print `design_line` in their JSON where they printed `option` or `options`, and a failed check now reads "the design line fails". A 0.4 (or 0.3) file opens as 0.5: its rolling-circle option (else its first other option) becomes the design line, its pool-lattice option the refined line, every other option is not kept, the stages after sizing keep their numbers but are marked stale, and the command line prints a note for each change. The map, the option table, the exports and the drawings still list the one line in the shape they had (a list of one), until the drawings read stored stations (ticket 10).
