- **The weir search can pay for the land its pools flood: `flood_cost_per_ha`, and a hard limit `max_above_bank_m`.** The cascade priced cut and structures only. A higher pool always cuts less, so it took the largest lift offered at almost every weir, and on a river with low banks the pools stood many metres above bank-full. Two new optimise settings, both off by default (a design that sets neither gives the same weirs and numbers, byte for byte):
  - `flood_cost_per_ha` (`owd optimise --flood-cost-per-ha X`) is the cost of one hectare of land a pool floods outside the river polygon, in the units of the cut cost. It is added to the cost of each (site, level) the search weighs, so the search trades cut against flooded land. A starting value is 3,000 per hectare; the value is the engineer's to set.
  - `max_above_bank_m` (`--max-above-bank X`) allows no pool more than X metres above bank-full anywhere in its reach. With no cascade inside the limit the stage fails and says which reach blocks it and what would unblock it (more sites, smaller lifts, a higher limit).
  - Flooded land is ground outside the river polygon, below the pool level, joined to the river through other ground below the level, nearest to the pool's own length of the design line and within `flood_distance_m` of it (`--flood-distance`, default 1,500 m), on a grid of `flood_cell_m` cells (default 25 m). A pool whose water reaches the edge of that grid or of the DEM is flagged, and its area is a lower bound. One function gives it: `owd_core::optimise::flood::FloodField`.
  - Bank-full is an estimate from the DEM: in each 500 m of line, the 25th percentile of the ground in the cells that touch the river polygon, on each side of the line, and the lower of the two sides.
  - With a setting on, or with `--flood-report` (which leaves the search unchanged), `results.cascade.flood` gives, for each pool and in total, the flooded hectares and their cost, the height above bank-full at the lower and upper end and at the worst place, and the depth over the DEM's river level at each end. `owd optimise` prints the table and the app shows it under the Optimise row, with an input for each setting.
  - Both settings need a DEM: without one a flood cost above 0, or a limit, is refused in plain words.
  - The volumes stage is unchanged: the flood cost is in the weir search only.
