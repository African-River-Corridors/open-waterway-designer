- **A river polygon with an unclosed ring now reads as the closed ring.** Before, on a synthetic river 200 m wide and 1,000 m long with one island (100 m x 40 m) across a 61 m wide fairway, an island ring left unclosed gave one cut piece of 61,000 m2 (the whole fairway); it now gives 4,000 m2, the same as the closed ring. The same fix applies wherever the polygon is repaired or buffered (the plan cut, the water-aware line's navigable water). Polygons whose rings are closed do not move: the full test suite, with its recorded numbers, passes unchanged.
