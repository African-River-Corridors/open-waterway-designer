- **Constraints: no-go areas and the cut-rate multiplier (spec 0017, ticket 13).** A design can hold constraints (`inputs.constraints`, schema `schema/stages/inputs.constraints.1.schema.json`): no-go areas (polygons with holes in the project CRS, each with a unique name and a kind) and a cut-rate multiplier raster, referenced like the DEM by path, hash, CRS and nodata, whose cell value multiplies the cut rate in that cell (no data means 1). `owd constraints DOC --no-go FILE.geojson --multiplier FILE.tif` sets them, through the same session call as the web app's (`set_no_go`, `set_cut_rate_multiplier`). Every design line reports `constraints_touched`: each no-go area and each stretch of hard-exclusion ground (a multiplier cell at or above 1e6, walked over the raster at half its cell size) its channel footprint enters, with the chainage from and to, the area touched and the footprint width tested (the straight bottom width only: cut slopes and bend widening are not tested). A line of any method that enters one fails the check `no_go_clear`: it is saved and marked failed, the CLI prints what it enters, and `--strict` exits 1. The re-route treats a no-go area, and a multiplier cell at or above 1e6, as impassable, weighs its cut by the multiplier, and refuses a line that still enters a no-go area (`ALIGN_NO_GO_CROSSED`) or hard-exclusion ground (`ALIGN_HARD_EXCLUSION_CROSSED`). `owd design-line` now follows `--strict` too. The volumes stage stores the cut weighted by the multiplier beside the plain cut, per station, per reach and in total; the cost prices dredge and dry cut on the weighted volumes and says which it used (`cut_volumes`). Refusals carry codes: `CONSTRAINTS_INVALID`, `CONSTRAINTS_NAME_MISSING`, `CONSTRAINTS_NAME_DUPLICATE`, `CONSTRAINTS_KIND_MISSING`, `CONSTRAINTS_POLYGON_INVALID`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_UNREADABLE`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_CRS`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_VALUE`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_CHANGED`, `ALIGN_NO_MULTIPLIER` (every method, when a named raster is not there), `OPTIMISE_NO_MULTIPLIER`, `VOLUMES_NO_MULTIPLIER`. A design with no constraints opens, saves and designs as before: no design number moves.
