- **Constraints: no-go areas and the cut-rate multiplier (spec 0017, ticket 13).** A design can hold constraints (`inputs.constraints`, schema `schema/stages/inputs.constraints.1.schema.json`): no-go areas (polygons with holes in the project CRS, each with a unique name and a kind) and a cut-rate multiplier raster, referenced like the DEM by path, hash, CRS and nodata, whose cell value multiplies the cut rate in that cell (no data means 1). `owd constraints DOC --no-go FILE.geojson --multiplier FILE.tif` sets them, through the same session call as the web app's (`set_no_go`, `set_cut_rate_multiplier`). Every design line reports `constraints_touched`: each no-go area its channel footprint enters, with the chainage from and to and the area touched. The re-route treats a no-go area, and a multiplier cell at or above 1e6, as impassable, weighs its cut by the multiplier, and refuses a line that still enters a no-go area (`ALIGN_NO_GO_CROSSED`). The volumes stage stores the cut weighted by the multiplier beside the plain cut, per station, per reach and in total; the cost prices dredge and dry cut on the weighted volumes and says which it used (`cut_volumes`). Refusals carry codes: `CONSTRAINTS_INVALID`, `CONSTRAINTS_NAME_MISSING`, `CONSTRAINTS_NAME_DUPLICATE`, `CONSTRAINTS_KIND_MISSING`, `CONSTRAINTS_POLYGON_INVALID`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_UNREADABLE`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_CRS`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_VALUE`, `CONSTRAINTS_RASTER_CHANGED`, `ALIGN_NO_MULTIPLIER`, `VOLUMES_NO_MULTIPLIER`. A design with no constraints opens, saves and designs as before: no design number moves.
