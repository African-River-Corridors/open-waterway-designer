- **The corridor can be a real limit of the pool search: `corridor_limit` (OPT-1).** The corridor (`corridor_half_m`, 350 m by default, either side of the baseline) only fed the search's heuristic. The search itself was bounded by the rectangle round the corridor, so lines were found well outside it. With the new optimise setting `corridor_limit` on, no state of the lattice, no point of a primitive and no point of a closing path may lie outside the corridor, read at the nearest grid cell. A pool whose closing path would leave the corridor is unclosed, and goes the way an unclosed pool goes today: its search end moves, or it keeps the baseline. A pool with no line inside the corridor at all keeps the baseline with the reason "the search found no line inside the corridor", and the design line carries a warning that names the pool. Set it with `owd optimise --corridor-limit` (`--corridor-limit false` turns it off), or with the tick box "Keep the line inside the corridor" in the app's Optimise row. The default is false: the setting is then not written, and every design gives the same line and numbers. The baseline is the alignment's design line when the design has one, else the river centreline: the corridor is centred on that line. On a synthetic open plain with a 120 m corridor the free search ran up to 301 m from the baseline; held, it stays within 120 m and is 6.5 % longer.
