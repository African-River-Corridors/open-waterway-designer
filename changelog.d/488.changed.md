- **Water-aware alignment whenever there is a river polygon, and `owd align --use-water`.** With `use_water` unset, the rolling circle keeps to the water whenever the design has a river polygon, whether the user gave it or the import built it from the two banks. `owd align --use-water true|false` sets the rolling circle's `use_water` and stores it in the design's alignment settings, as the app shell's new `set_alignment_settings` does; `owd run` keeps it. The rolling-circle option records the value it used, also when it came from the default.
