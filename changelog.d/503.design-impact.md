- **At a bend the water-aware rolling circle cannot fit, the line is now the better of the fairway fit's line and the plain rolling circle's.** Before, the fit's line was always taken, even where it left the water far more than the plain line did. Now the line that holds R_min on the default chord wins (else the one that breaks it least), then the one with less channel width outside the water, and a fit that makes the line cross itself is never taken. The bend is still reported infeasible, with its stretch. On a synthetic sine river of wavelength 1,200 m and amplitude 152 m, with water 40 m wide at each apex and 120 m between, a channel needing 45.5 m, rolled at R_min 240 m (step 10 m, two passes, both sides), over 6,000 m: the width missing from the water (summed over 5 m stations) falls from 137,215 m2 to 385 m2, equal to the plain line's 385 m2, and the radius on the 80 m chord goes from 784.0 m to 240.0 m, equal to the plain line's (the fit had straightened the bends out of the water). With `--use-water false`, and wherever a bend takes rung 1, 2 or 3, the line does not move.
