- **At a bend the water-aware rolling circle cannot fit, the line is now the better of the fairway fit's line and the plain rolling circle's.** Before, the fit's line was always taken, even where it left the water far more than the plain line did. Now the line that holds R_min on the default chord wins (else the one that breaks it least), then the one with less channel width outside the water, and a fit that makes the line cross itself is never taken. The bend is still reported infeasible, with its stretch. On a synthetic sine river of wavelength 1,200 m and amplitude 152 m, with water 40 m wide at each apex and 120 m between, a channel needing 45.5 m, rolled at R_min 240 m (step 10 m, two passes, both sides), over 6,000 m: the width missing from the water (summed over 5 m stations) falls from 137,215 m2 to 385 m2, equal to the plain line's 385 m2, and the radius on the 80 m chord goes from 784.0 m to 240.0 m, equal to the plain line's (the fit had straightened the bends out of the water). With `--use-water false`, and wherever a bend takes rung 1, 2 or 3, the line does not move.

**What moves on the repository's own cases.** On every bend that takes rung 4 here, the fit's line was worse than the plain line on both measures, so the water-aware line now equals the plain line there; with `--use-water false` nothing moves. Two cases:

- The use-water test river (two sine banks 150 m apart, 600 m amplitude, 1.2 km wavelength; R_min 255 m, chord 85 m): all 10 bends take rung 4. Radius on the chord per bend, before and after: 198.9 to 241.9 m (every one below R_min) becomes 252.8 to 253.7 m (the plain line's; it passes the radius check). Channel width outside the water per bend window, summed over 5 m stations: 17,937 to 25,628 m2 for the plain line against 18,080 to 25,743 m2 for the fit, so the fit also had 0.1 to 2.2 % more. Plain mode is the same before and after.
- The web example river at vessel class CEMT_Va (R_min 330 m, chord 110 m): 3 bends take rung 4. Radius per bend: 210.6, 235.4 and 221.9 m become 329.1, 329.2 and 328.5 m. Width outside the water: 11,601, 16,365 and 10,971 m2 (plain) against 12,167, 16,483 and 10,986 m2 (fit).

The water is much narrower than the channel on both cases (about 20,000 m2 missing in each window either way), so the width measure is nearly a tie and the radius decides. Tests that needed a case where the water mode changes the line now use a river with room in the water (350 m amplitude, 1.8 km wavelength, banks 360 m apart): the water-aware line holds R_min and fits the water; the plain line has a 171 m radius against 255 m. Two drawing tests that needed a tight bend now draw the unrepaired centreline option, which still has one.

**What this does not claim.** The rule is bend by bend. On the use-water test river, the water-aware line over the whole design can still break R_min where the plain line holds it, between bend windows (issue #514, open). The tests here prove the better-of-two rule on the synthetic rivers they build, not on every river.
