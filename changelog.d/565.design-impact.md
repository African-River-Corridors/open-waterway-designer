- **The pool lattice's line changes only where a pool's search could not close on its end pose.** A pool that closes is searched and spliced exactly as before, so its line, cost and joins do not move (a test splices each pool's own line by hand and compares). A pool that falls back (`goal_closed` false, with `close_goal` true) is now searched again on a moved end, or keeps the baseline when its join would break R_min or cross itself, so the joined line, its radius readings and the volumes measured along it move there, towards a line that holds R_min at its joins. The lattice option's details gain `end_moved_from_m` and `start_moved_from_m` per pool and `self_crossing_m` for the line (issue #565).
