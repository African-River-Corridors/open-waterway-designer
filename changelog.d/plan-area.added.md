- **With no DEM the optimise stage runs on plan area (OPT-1).** The stage refused a design with no DEM. It now runs: with no ground to cut it chooses no weirs and no pool levels and needs no water level, and it searches the whole design line (in pieces, like any long pool) for the least channel footprint outside the river polygon, in m2, plus the length cost. The land weight is `land_weight` when it is set, else 1. The result says plainly that it was optimised on plan area and not on volume: `results.optimised_on` is `"plan_area"`, the lattice's details carry `optimised_on`, `optimised_on_note`, `land_weight` and `land_weight_defaulted`, the design line carries a warning, `owd optimise` prints a line, and the app shows a note in the Optimise row. `results.pools` and `results.profile` are empty and `cascade.weirs` is empty, so the later stages read the water level of the inputs. The volumes stage is not changed and still refuses without a DEM. A design that names a DEM whose file is missing is refused as before. A design with a DEM gives the same bytes as before.
