- **The reference line, the envelope sweep and the fits answer are typed stages (spec 0017, ticket 7).** The reference line is a pure function (`reference_line_stage`) stored as a stage record under `stages.reference_line`: its parameters as settings, its line and stretches as results (schema `schema/stages/reference_line.1.schema.json`), and as `depends_on` the hash of its whole input and the project CRS, so a new centreline or new bank notes mark it stale; the bed is centred on it while `line_basis`, the hash of what shapes the line (the banks, the river polygon, the project CRS and the parameters, the formula used before), still matches. A file that holds it as `inputs.reference_line` opens with it moved there: a fresh line stays fresh, and the volumes that read the bed centred on it keep their hash. The envelope sweep's answer is the typed `EnvelopeResult` (`schema/stages/envelope.1.schema.json`) and the fits answer the typed `FitsAnswer` (`schema/stages/fits.1.schema.json`), each checked against its type before it is stored; their `depends_on` is the hash of the typed `EnvelopeIn` and `FitsIn`, which now also hold the banks and the project CRS (and, for a full answer, the sizing and volumes settings and the optimise pool levels). An answer stored before this change opens stale, and opening the file says so and what to run. Their numbers do not change. The fits record stays where it was, at the top level of the document.
