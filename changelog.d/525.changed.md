- **The reference line and the envelope sweep are typed stages (spec 0017, ticket 7, in part).** The reference line is a pure function (`reference_line_stage`) stored as a stage record under `stages.reference_line`: its parameters as settings, its line and stretches as results (schema `schema/stages/reference_line.1.schema.json`), and as `depends_on` the hash of the input it read. A file that holds it as `inputs.reference_line` opens with it moved there, stale. The envelope sweep's answer is the typed `EnvelopeResult` (schema `schema/stages/envelope.1.schema.json`), checked before it is stored, and its `depends_on` is the hash of the typed `EnvelopeIn`. Neither has a hash of its own any more. Their numbers do not change.
