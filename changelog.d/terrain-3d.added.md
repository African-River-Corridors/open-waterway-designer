- **3D view of the DEM (MAP-2).** The web app's map has a **3D** button beside the Layers button. With a DEM in memory it tilts the map to 60° and draws the DEM as a surface, with every ticked layer laid on it (imagery, river polygon, design line, stretches). Right-drag or Ctrl-drag turns and tilts; the zoom buttons now have a compass, and a click on it turns the map north up. A **Height** list sets the vertical exaggeration: 1×, 2× (the default, for flat river country), 5× or 10×. With no DEM the button is off and says "load a DEM first". 3D off brings back the flat, north-up map. The Layers list has a new **Terrain shading** tick (off at first): over the imagery it shades the slopes; with Imagery unticked it shows the DEM itself, tinted by height (low ground dark, high ground pale) and shaded, in 2D or 3D. The terrain comes from the DEM already in the browser: the engine cuts it into 256 px Terrarium tiles on demand (`owd_format::design::terrain_tile`, bilinear, through the DEM's own CRS), the map reads them under a scheme of its own (`owd-dem://`), and nothing is sent to any server. Ground the DEM does not cover is drawn at the DEM's median height, and the DEM's edge eases to it over an apron a tenth of the DEM wide, so there is no hole and no cliff at the edge; a nodata hole inside the DEM is flat at that height. 3D on or off and the exaggeration are remembered on this machine (browser storage), never in the design file: the design file and every export are unchanged. The desktop app does not have the 3D view yet: its button is off and says so.
