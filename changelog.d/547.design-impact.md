- **A joined pool-lattice line holds R_min where it broke it.** On a synthetic sine river (amplitude 250 m, wavelength 1,600 m, a river 80 m wide at -12 m in ground at +3 m, channel half-width 25 m, R_min 340 m, one 3,200 m pool), the joined line had a minimum radius of 261.9 m on the check chord; it is now 336.6 m, the pool's own lattice line. The lattice line costs more than the baseline there (about 58,000 against 0 in the scorer's units): that cost is now paid, because the cheaper line breaks R_min. Where the baseline already holds R_min and is cheaper it is kept, as before. `n_replaced` counts used pools: it can fall where a converged pool kept its baseline. The repository's own cases do not move: the full suite, with its recorded numbers, passes unchanged.
