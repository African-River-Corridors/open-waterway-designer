- **The water stage (spec 0017, ticket 8).** `water_stage::water(&WaterIn) -> Result<DesignWater, StageError>` is a pure function, tested alone from fixtures (`tests/fixtures/water/`). It gives the water level along the design line on design-line chainage (from the river mouth when one is stated): a point every `step_m` (the volumes station step by default) and two at each weir or change of source, each with the river chainage it was read at, its level, where the level comes from (`given`, `pool` or `open_river`), a pool's minimum and maximum level when the pool has them (issue 532), and whether it is held flat beyond the profile's ends. The volumes stage stores it as `stages.water` (schema `schema/stages/water.1.schema.json`), with the hash of its input as `depends_on`; its levels are the ones the volumes walk reads. Refusals by code: `WATER_NO_RIVER`, `WATER_NO_DESIGN_LINE`, `WATER_NO_LEVEL`, `WATER_NO_OPEN_RIVER_LEVEL`, `WATER_PROFILE_INVALID`, `WATER_POOL_INVALID`, `WATER_POOL_LEVELS_INVALID`, `WATER_POOLS_OVERLAP`, `WATER_SETTING`. No design number moves.
