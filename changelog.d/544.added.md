- **A design sanity check: the run warns when the design line crosses itself, when a bend is tighter than R_min, and when a station's cut is far above its neighbours'.** After `owd align` and `owd volumes` (and `owd run`), each warning is a line on stderr and an entry under `sanity` on the option (alignment) or the volumes row. A warning never fails a check and never changes an exit code. A cut is far above its neighbours when it is more than 3 times the median of the 5 stations each side and above it by more than 100 m2.
