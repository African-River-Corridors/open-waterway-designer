- **Bank checks at the door.** Every import that brings a bank runs the bank checks once both banks are in the document, and reports what fails: one line per failed check, with how many stations of 20 m fail and between which chainages. `owd import` prints the lines (`warnings` in its `--json` result); the app shows them with its other warnings. The checks report only: they neither repair nor refuse the banks. Banks made from imagery that cross are reported as a warning.
