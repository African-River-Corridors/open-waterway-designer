- **A pool longer than 6 km is now searched as pieces, so its lattice line moves.** A pool no longer than `max_pool_piece_m` (6,000 m) runs exactly as before: one search, the same line and cost (a test checks the result is equal with and without the split). A longer pool is cut into pieces of about 6 km, each pinned to the baseline's pose at its boundaries, so its line passes through those poses where the one search could route away from them; its cost can rise a little for that, and where the one search hit its cap the pool now gets a line where it kept the baseline. On the tests' synthetic sine river (amplitude 250 m, wavelength 1,600 m, river 30 m wide, R_min 340 m, pitch 24 m, cell 8 m), with the search limit at 300,000 expansions, a 31 km pool hit the limit and kept the baseline; it is now six pieces of 4.8 to 5.8 km, each converged, with every piece join holding R_min. The lattice option's pool records gain `piece`, `expansions` and `hit_cap`, and `n_replaced` counts pieces. The design-impact scenarios do not move: the `optimise_pools` example's pools are shorter than 6 km.
