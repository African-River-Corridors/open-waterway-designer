- **Buildings, Places and Settlements on the map (BLD-1).** Three new ticks in Layers, greyed "none loaded" until a file is loaded. Buildings (off by default): a density further out, dots from zoom 12 and footprints from zoom 14, in 2D and draped in 3D; with Pool water on, the buildings the water covers at least 0.3 m deep turn orange and the Land table gains a "Buildings flooded" column per pool; the edge shows the cut (in it, near it, on a severed meander island). Places (on by default): a mark for each kind (community, bridge, quay, loading site, ferry, intake, structure, other) and the name, drawn by the page with no font server, the larger place kept where two names collide, a legend of the kinds; a bridge or a quay that gives its size gets a placeholder block, "placeholder model, not a design". Settlements (off by default): a thin outline and light fill. A click opens a card: area, confidence, ground, the pool's water and depth, distance to the design line and its chainage, the cut; for a settlement the share the pool water covers. The ticks never change the design.
