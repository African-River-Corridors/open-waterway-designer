- **The bank checks move to the core.** The pure bank checks (side, crossing, width) and the station helpers they read the banks with now live in `owd_core::banks` (`check`, `stations`). `owd check-banks` takes them from there; `owd-reach` keeps only the repairs and calls the moved code; `owd-format`'s `midline` re-exports the helpers under their old names. No check changes what it reports.
