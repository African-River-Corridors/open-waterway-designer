- **Pool-lattice lines meet at their pool joins without breaking R_min.** Each pool's search now ends on the goal pose exactly, position and heading, through a closing path: a Dubins curve of R_min (arc, straight, arc), costed like any lattice edge and tried from every state within 3 × R_min of the goal (`LatticeConfig::close_goal`, `close_reach_m`). The next pool starts on the same pose, so two pools meet tangentially. Before, a pool's line stopped within half a pitch and one heading bin of its goal, and the splice joined the two with a straight step, so the join broke R_min though each pool held it. Only when no closing path is found does the search fall back to the old tolerance (`LatticeResult::goal_closed` false). Every join is now read for R_min on the joined line and reported per join (`PoolsResult::joins`, and `details.joins` on the `lattice` option); a join that breaks R_min also gives a sanity warning, which `owd optimise` now prints (issue #6).
