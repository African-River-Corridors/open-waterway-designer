- **The plan cut in the document, on both surfaces.** `owd plan-cut DOC [--min-piece-m2 M] [--geojson FILE] [--wgs84]` works out the plan cut of each design line option (the fairway at the water surface, less the river polygon) and keeps it in `stages.plan_cut`: the area, by bank, the pieces and the cut polygons. It needs no terrain and no water level, and says "plan only". It has its own status, like the largest-vessel sweep, and the run loop never runs it. The app shell runs the same (`AppState::run_plan_cut`); the schema gains the optional `stages.plan_cut`.
