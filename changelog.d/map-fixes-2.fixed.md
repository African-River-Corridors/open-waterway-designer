- **The pool water's level now steps on the weir's cross-line, to the metre.** On a large DEM the flood mask's cells are tens of metres, and the water took each cell's pool, so the level could step up to a cell away from the weir, where no weir was drawn. Where two pools meet, each pixel now takes the pool of its nearest point of the design line, worked out at the pixel. The legend says how many steps the water level has and how many weirs are drawn ("18 level steps, 18 weirs drawn"), and warns when they differ. A weir's mark no longer fades when the 3D terrain covers it. The weir card says where the level changes, and says that a river loop the design line has left (a bypassed meander) is not modelled apart. A step whose line has no direction there still gets its weir.
