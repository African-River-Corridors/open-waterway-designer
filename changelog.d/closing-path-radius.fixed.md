- **The pool lattice's line reads its true radius through the closing path.** The line the lattice returns is a polyline, and points resampled along its chords sit inside the circle, so an arc in coarse steps reads tighter than it is. The closing path added for issue #6 was sampled every 10 m, and on the golden hill case (R_min 150 m) its R_min arc read 144.5 m on 25 m vertices, under the 0.99 bound of the Python golden test. The geometry was never tighter than R_min: every three vertices of the line lie on a circle of at least R_min, junctions included. Every arc of the returned line, lattice primitive or closing path, is now sampled in steps of at most 2 m (`LINE_ARC_STEP_M`). The search, its costs and its decisions do not change. Also, the Python `LatticeConfig` notes that `close_goal` and `close_reach_m` act in Python as in Rust, since the Python lattice runs the Rust search, with a test (issue #557).
