- **A radius inside the R_min reading tolerance is not a constraint stretch.** A radius stretch is now listed only when its radius fails `radius_ok`, the 2 % tolerance the R_min check already uses; before, any reading below R_min by any amount was listed. On a synthetic straight river with a design line whose bend reads 0.04 m under R_min (R_min 330 m), the stretch list went from 1 radius stretch (verdict `navigable-with-works`) to none (verdict `navigable-as-is`). A radius more than 2 % under R_min is a stretch as before. Width and depth stretches are unchanged, and no volume changes.
