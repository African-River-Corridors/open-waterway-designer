- **The river stage can orient the river from its mouth (spec 0017, ticket 4, the stage part).** `RiverIn` takes a `mouth` (a point, or `downstream`, decided by the water level profile or by the ground levels at the two ends), and the stage turns the centreline, the banks and the water profile so that chainage 0 is at the mouth. A mouth that cannot be decided is refused with `RIVER_MOUTH_UNKNOWN`. The document does not record a mouth yet, so the stages that have not moved onto the river stage read their lines as stored, and no existing result changes.
