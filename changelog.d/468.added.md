- **The longest vessel for each beam.** `owd envelope --beams 4,6,8 [--length-search MIN:MAX:STEP]` finds, for each beam, the longest single-hull vessel the river takes as it stands (by bisection over the lengths, on the existing size runs), whether the search's own bound stopped it, and the stretches that stop the next length. The answers are kept in `stages.envelope.results.by_beam`; a sweep with no beam is as before. The app shell runs it with `AppState::run_envelope_with`.
