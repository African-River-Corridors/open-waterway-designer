- **Map layers (MAP-1).** The map has a Layers button under its zoom buttons. It opens a list of tick boxes: River polygon, Centreline, Bank lines, Design line, Constraint stretches, Station marks, Imagery. A tick shows or hides its layer at once. A layer the design does not have is greyed and says "none loaded". The ticks are remembered on this machine (browser storage), never in the design file; everything is on at first. The legend lists only what the design has and what is ticked on, and now has keys for the river polygon, the bank lines, the centreline and the station marks. The river polygon also has an outline: a pale line over a dark casing, which reads on bright and on dark imagery.
