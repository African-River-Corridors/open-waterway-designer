- **One design line per run moves the numbers of two kinds of run, both where a second line used to sit beside the first.** (1) The re-route now starts from the river centreline: there is no rolling-circle line beside it to start from. On the web example's synthetic river (2.4 km, CEMT IV, PIANC WG 141 quality B, draught 2.5 m), `owd align --method rolling-circle` then `owd align --method reroute` gave a re-route line of 3,013.7 m with dredge 266,953 m3 and dry cut 440,094 m3; it now gives 2,959.9 m, 264,858 m3 and 484,885 m3, the same as a re-route run alone, which does not move. (2) The pool lattice starts from the alignment's design line, whatever its method; it started from the rolling-circle option, else the river centreline. On the same river with `owd optimise --sites 1200`: after a re-route, the lattice line was 3,025.1 m with dredge 255,192 m3 and dry cut 488,341 m3, and is now 3,002.3 m, 249,543 m3 and 496,805 m3; after a given line (here the rolling circle's own line, given with `owd design-line`), it was the same 3,025.1 m, 255,192 m3 and 488,341 m3 (from the river centreline), and is now 3,029.1 m, 263,144 m3 and 483,033 m3, the same as after the rolling circle itself. A design that runs one method, or the rolling circle then the lattice, gives the same line and the same volumes, number for number (checked against main at 8e62260). The design-impact scenarios call the core, not the document, and do not move.
