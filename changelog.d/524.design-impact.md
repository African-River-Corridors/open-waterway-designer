- **One design line per run moves two numbers, both where two methods used to run on one design.** (1) The re-route now starts from the river centreline: there is no rolling-circle line beside it to start from. On the web example's synthetic river (2.4 km, CEMT IV, PIANC WG 141 quality B, draught 2.5 m), `owd align --method rolling-circle` then `owd align --method reroute` gave a re-route line of 3,013.7 m with dredge 266,953 m3 and dry cut 440,094 m3; it now gives 2,959.9 m, 264,858 m3 and 484,885 m3, the same as a re-route run alone, which does not move. (2) The pool lattice starts from the alignment's design line, whatever its method; it started from the rolling-circle option, else the river centreline. On the same river, a re-route then `owd optimise --sites 1200` gave a lattice line of 3,039.3 m with dredge 244,835 m3 and dry cut 535,104 m3; it now gives 3,028.8 m, 213,012 m3 and 645,445 m3. A design that runs one method, with or without the pool lattice after a rolling circle, gives the same line and the same volumes, number for number (rolling circle, re-route, centreline, rolling circle then lattice, all checked against main). The design-impact scenarios call the core, not the document, and do not move.
