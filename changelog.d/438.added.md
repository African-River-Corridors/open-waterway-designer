- **The bank dataset type.** `owd_format::design::banks::BankDataset` is one typed view of the banks of a design: the two lines, the river polygon with its holes, and what the bank source says (source, accuracy, low-confidence stretches), with a SHA-256 hash of all of it. The document gains an optional `inputs.banks` member for the source, `accuracy_m` and `low_confidence`; the bank lines stay where they are, and an old document opens unchanged. `Session::bank_dataset` reads it; `Session::set_bank_accuracy` records the accuracy (a new value makes the stages after sizing stale). No stage reads the dataset yet.
