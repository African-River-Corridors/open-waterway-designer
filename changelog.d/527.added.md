- **The volumes stage stores its stations, its volumes by reach and its totals (spec 0017, ticket 9).** `volumes_stage::volumes_stage(&VolumesIn, terrain) -> Result<Volumes, StageError>` is a pure function, tested alone; the session's volumes run walks the line with the same code and stores the same `Volumes` as `stages.volumes.results.sections` (schema `schema/stages/volumes.1.schema.json`), beside the numbers it always stored. Every station keeps its numbers (chainage, place, heading, half-width, water level, invert, depth, the bed on the line, the areas by kind, the tallest face, the benches), which is all the long section draws. The section profile (offsets, terrain, wet, cut face) is kept only at the stations a cross-section sheet draws by default: the station nearest each multiple of 500 m, the middles of the tight bends, and the worst station of each constraint stretch. The volumes by reach follow the water stage's stretches (one per source and pool) and add up to the totals. An island in the river polygon is dry cut, not dredge (tested; no change was needed). Refusals: `VOLUMES_NO_LINE`, `VOLUMES_NO_WATER`, `VOLUMES_WALK`. On the web example the saved document grows from about 146 kB to about 745 kB. No design number moves.
