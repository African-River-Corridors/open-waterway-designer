- **Typed volumes settings.** The volumes stage's settings are a type, `owd_format::design::VolumesSettings`. A setting the stage does not know, from the caller or in the document, is refused by name before any work, where a misspelt key was accepted and written into the document before. `owd volumes --help` reads the defaults of `--station` and `--maxw` from the type instead of typing them again.
