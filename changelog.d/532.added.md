- **Given weirs and locks, pool levels and the structures seam (spec 0017, ticket 14).** A design can give its structures (`inputs.structures`, schema `schema/stages/inputs.structures.1.schema.json`): mode `given`, each item with a site (a river chainage from the mouth, or a point within half the channel width of the design line), the mean water level it holds upstream, its height, its cost (money, one currency), and an optional name and kind (`weir`, `lock`, `weir_and_lock`); and `pool_levels`, the allowances below and above each pool's level that give its minimum and maximum level (0.5 m each when not given, written out). `owd structures DOC --given FILE` sets them (`--clear` removes them), through the same session call as the web engine's `set_structures`. The volumes run places them (the stage `structures`, schema `schema/stages/structures.1.schema.json`): each structure's river and design-line chainage, upstream and downstream level, lift, and the height, footprint and cost the structures seam returns (the built-in pass-through returns what was given), and the pools between them with their design, minimum and maximum level. The water stage reads these pools, open river below the lowest structure, so its points carry the minimum and maximum levels. Two checks are saved with the volumes and fail under `--strict`: `navigation_depth` (the depth at each pool's minimum level against the design depth, at each stored station in a pool) and `flood_risk` (each pool's maximum level against the lowest bank at the stations that keep a section profile). The cost gains a `structures` item by reach and in total. Refusals carry codes: `WEIRS_INVALID`, `WEIRS_NO_STRUCTURES`, `WEIRS_ITEM_INVALID`, `WEIRS_MIXED_CURRENCY`, `WEIRS_POOL_LEVELS_INVALID`, `WEIRS_SITE_OFF_LINE`, `WEIRS_SITES_COINCIDE`, `WEIRS_LEVELS_NOT_STEPPED`, `WEIRS_NO_DOWNSTREAM_LEVEL`, `WEIRS_NO_RIVER`, `WEIRS_NO_DESIGN_LINE`, `WEIRS_SETTING`, `WEIRS_SEAM`, `COST_STRUCTURE_UNPLACED`. A design with no structures opens, saves and designs as before: no design number moves.
