- **A river polygon's islands are kept by the re-route, the optimise stage and the volumes stage.** They parsed the polygon again and dropped its holes, so an island counted as water. Now it is dry land: on a straight 2 km river with a 1 km island across the channel, the volumes stage's dredge falls from 494 606 m3 to 244 250 m3 and its dry cut rises from 0 to 250 356 m3 (the total is unchanged). A river polygon without islands gives the same numbers as before.
