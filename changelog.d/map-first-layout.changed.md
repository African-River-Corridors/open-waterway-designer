- **The app is map-first (UI-4).** The map now fills all the space beside the left panel. The design line (its verdict and the reason, dredge, dry cut, tallest face, minimum radius and checks) is a compact card in the left panel, under Stages, with the Checks list below it. The profile (the long section) and the cross-section are two floating panels over the map. They are see-through, so the imagery shows faintly behind them. Each has a title bar to drag it by, a corner to resize it by and a Close button, and Escape closes the one in focus. The toolbar's new **Profile** and **Section** buttons show and hide them, and a click on the design line on the map goes to the nearest station and shows both. With both hidden you see only the river. The Station slider is in the cross-section panel. Where you put the panels, and their size, is remembered on your machine and is never saved in a design. The constraint stretches are in a drawer under the map, closed until you open it from the **Constraint stretches** button in the left panel; picking a stretch still moves the map to it. No design number moves.
