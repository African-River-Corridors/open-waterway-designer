- **Large DEMs read in sections (COG-1).** A tiled GeoTIFF (a cloud-optimised GeoTIFF) is read a block at a time, at the detail asked for, with a bounded cache of decoded blocks, so a DEM's size no longer limits OWD. The reader takes classic TIFF and BigTIFF, 8, 16 and 32 bit integers and 32 or 64 bit floats, DEFLATE, LZW or no compression, the horizontal predictor (on floats too) and the floating-point one, and any internal overviews. A file it cannot read is refused by name, with what to do.
