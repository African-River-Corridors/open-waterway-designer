- **Large DEMs read in sections (COG-1).** A tiled GeoTIFF (a cloud-optimised GeoTIFF) of 64 MiB or more is read a block at a time, at the detail asked for, with a bounded cache of decoded blocks (about 256 MB; 128 MB in the browser; the terrain limit setting sets it), so a DEM's size no longer limits OWD. The reader takes classic TIFF and BigTIFF, 8, 16 and 32 bit integers and 32 or 64 bit floats, DEFLATE, LZW or no compression, the horizontal predictor (on floats too) and the floating-point one, and any internal overviews. Volumes and sections read full resolution; the optimise stage's search grid and flood field, the map's tiles, contours and the hillshade read the coarsest overview fine enough for them, and the optimise details say so (`ground_read_cell_m`). A file it cannot read is refused by name, with what to do. See `docs/large-dems.md`.
- **`owd dem info` and `owd dem prepare`.** `info` says what a GeoTIFF holds and whether OWD reads it in sections. `prepare` writes a sectioned copy (512 x 512 tiles, DEFLATE with a predictor, overviews by averaging that leaves nodata out, BigTIFF when needed, directories first), a band of rows at a time, never the whole raster in memory.
- **A large sectioned DEM is recorded by a quick check.** Its length, its header and directories, and a sample of its blocks (`inputs.dem.quick_check`), so checking it does not read the whole file. A design that records a full SHA-256 is checked as before, the file read a piece at a time. `owd project status --full` hashes every byte, with progress.
