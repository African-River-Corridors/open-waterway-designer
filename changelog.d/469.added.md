- **Follow the centreline.** A third alignment method, `owd align --method centreline`, adds the river centreline as it is (or the line built from the two banks) as a design line option to compare with, with no repair. Its radius is read as the rolling circle reads it, so a bend tighter than the minimum radius fails the check and says so; the command still succeeds, as the line is kept as it is. The option says `widening_floor: none`, and the core gains `widened_hb_from_shape_floor` with `WideningFloor`, so a later ticket can widen such bends by their own radius.
