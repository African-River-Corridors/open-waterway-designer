- **Contours from the DEM (MAP-3).** The Layers list of the web app's map has a new **Contours** tick (off at first; greyed and "none loaded" with no DEM). On, the map draws the contour lines of the DEM in memory: thin lines, and every fifth one an index contour, drawn heavier with its level in metres along it. The lines lie over the imagery or the terrain shading and under the design, in 2D and draped on the surface in 3D, pale on a soft dark edge so they read on bright or dark ground in either theme. Beside the tick, while it is on, an interval list: **Auto**, 1 m, 2 m, 5 m, 10 m or 20 m. Auto picks the interval from the zoom level (1 m from zoom 15 in, then 2 m, 5 m, 10 m, and 20 m from zoom 11 out), so lines on a 1-in-20 slope stay about 8 to 10 screen pixels apart and the map never floods. A chosen interval is drawn from one zoom level further out than Auto would pick it (1 m from zoom 14, 2 m from 13, 5 m from 12, 10 m from 11); further out the map draws the next coarser interval, and the legend says which interval is on the map. No contour is drawn further out than zoom 9. The legend lists the contours and the index contours when the tick is on. The lines are made in the browser, off the page's thread, one map tile at a time and only while the tick is on: the engine reads the DEM's own heights on the grid the terrain tiles use (`TileMaker::ground`, two pixels past the tile's edge so the lines of two tiles meet), traces every level by marching squares, joins the pieces into whole lines, smooths them lightly, and answers with a Mapbox Vector Tile (`owd_format::design::contours`) that the map reads under a scheme of its own (`owd-contour://`). Every level is a whole multiple of the interval; ground the DEM does not know (off the DEM, a nodata cell) has no line. The last 128 tiles are kept in memory. Nothing is sent to any server. The tick and the interval are remembered on this machine (browser storage), never in the design file: the design file and every export are unchanged. The desktop app does not have contours yet: its tick is off and says "web app only for now".
